## Brand & Style

This design system expresses safety, psychological warmth, organic balance, and contemporary editorial prestige. Built for individuals seeking therapeutic support, self-actualization, and emotional healing, the visual narrative avoids the clinical austerity of conventional medical portals in favor of a calming sanctuary. 

The aesthetic is an intentional fusion of **Organic Glassmorphism** and **Editorial Warmth**:
- Translucent veil layers with delicate backdrop blurs suggest personal introspection and vulnerability treated with utmost care.
- Breathing room and generous vertical rhythm reflect clarity of thought and unhurried mental space.
- Warm linen undertones harmonize with marine sage greens to generate physiological tranquility and somatic grounding.
- Micro-interactions feel natural and fluid, evoking the feeling of a calm exhalation rather than rigid digital feedback.

## Colors

The palette translates the seafoam spiral motif into an evocative therapeutic identity. Rather than stark black and clinical white, the interface rests on a canvas of warm linen (`#FAF8F5`), enveloped in deep sea slate (`#243330`) for high-contrast legibility that remains soft on the eyes.

- **Primary (`#74B6A6` - Aqua Marine):** Represents growth, renewal, and psychological balance. Reserved for key calls to action, active indicators, and high-impact focal accents.
- **Secondary (`#2B4B48` - Deep Cypress):** Provides anchoring gravity, structural elegance, and authoritative distinction for key headings and prominent UI surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#D1E5E0` - Soft Pale Mist):** Functions as a delicate transitional wash for secondary cards, pill badges, and soft highlights.
- **Neutral (`#243330` - Warm Slate):** Delivers rich typographic contrast without the aggressive glare of pure black.

Use translucent layers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) over subtle gradient underlays to produce the signature organic glass appearance.

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts the empathetic literary quality of **Playfair Display** with the clean, highly legible geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display lends editorial sensitivity, human authenticity, and timeless care to reflective statements, service names, and section intros. It evokes personal journals and comforting literature.
- **Body & Functional UI:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures crystalline readability, accessible character shapes, and neutral reliability across both long-form psychoeducational guidance and compact interactive modules.
- Generous line heights are maintained across all levels to create visual cadence and relieve cognitive tension.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a maximum readable content width of 1240px to ensure intimacy and structured pacing. 

- **Grid Architecture:** 12 columns with 1.5rem gutters on desktop, collapsing to 6 columns on tablet (breakpoint: 768px), and 4 columns on mobile (breakpoint: 480px).
- **Rhythm & Breathing Space:** Outer canvas margins (`3.5rem` desktop, `1.25rem` mobile) offer deep, uncluttered padding that frames the therapeutic journey.
- **Section Pacing:** Major sections use alternating structural alignments—offset narrative headers alongside media cards—deliberately breaking rigid corporate formulas while retaining clear scanning paths.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ethereal, atmospheric elevation rather than stark drop shadows, relying on layered translucent surfaces and ambient glow effects:

- **Organic Glass Planes:** Key surface containers utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` paired with a soft base (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) and a delicate top-lit rim highlight (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`).
- **Ethereal Shadows:** Shadows are extra-diffused and tinted with oceanic slate (`rgba(36, 51, 48, 0.04) 0px 14px 34px -4px, rgba(116, 182, 166, 0.08) 0px 8px 18px -2px`), giving floating elements an organic, weightless poise.
- **Interactive Depth Lift:** On hover, interactive cards smoothly shift their Y-translation (`-4px`) while expanding the ambient aqua blur (`rgba(116, 182, 166, 0.16) 0px 20px 40px`), mimicking gentle tactile uplift.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded** shape language (`roundedness: 2` with standard radii scaling to `1.5rem` on cards and dialogs). 

- **Organic Contours:** Curves reflect natural forms (smooth beach stones, leaf geometry, and water currents), completely avoiding harsh 90-degree corners.
- **Pill Attributes:** Badges, chip tags, and primary CTAs leverage full pill shaping (`9999px`) to create frictionless, safe-feeling targets for user contact.
- **Image Treatment:** Photography and clinical vignettes are framed with generous curvature (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem) and soft inner vignettes to maintain visual gentleness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill radius (`rounded-full`), saturated aqua marine (`#74B6A6`) background, deep slate text (`#2B4B48`) in medium weight (`label-lg`), with a subtle inner glow. Hover triggers a warm swell (`#62A494`) and an ethereal aqua shadow bloom.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Translucent linen fill with a semi-opaque boundary (`border: 1px solid rgba(43, 75, 72, 0.15)`), shifting on interaction to pale aqua tint (`rgba(209, 229, 224, 0.35)`).

### Cards & Glass Panels
- Built using organic glassmorphism: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` surface, framed by an ultra-thin highlight border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` on top, fading to `rgba(43, 75, 72, 0.05)` at the bottom).
- Internal padding matches `space-lg` (2rem) for spacious clarity.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Backgrounds set in quiet linen frost (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), contoured with `rounded-md` (0.75rem).
- Inactive borders use `border-subtle`; on focus, inputs reveal a prominent dual-ring halo in soft seafoam (`#74B6A6` at 30% spread) with dark teal typography, eliminating clinical severity.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill-shaped modules (`9999px`) tinted in pale mint mist (`#D1E5E0`) with deep cypress text (`#2B4B48`).
- Used to identify therapeutic areas (e.g., *Ansiedad*, *Autoestima*, *Terapia de Pareja*) with calm distinction.

### Process & Step Milestones
- Step indicators render numerical indexes (`01`, `02`) in elegant medium-weight Playfair Display paired with a soft circular backdrop, seamlessly connected by subtle, flowing vertical thread lines.
