## Direction contract

THESIS: Inala's site is a Camino credential for the first steps of therapy: the secular waymarking of the Camino Francés that crosses Pamplona (yellow arrows, blue tile markers, stamped boxes) carries the visitor from doubt to a first appointment. It refuses the category default of beige ground, sage accents, italic serif and stock hands.

OWN-WORLD: cool stone-grey ground; credential cardstock white with hairline printed boxes (casillas); Camino blue as the committed field (hero, mojón footer, selected bands); arrow yellow reserved for every action and every "next step"; Inala's spiral as the practice stamp in aqua ink, slightly rotated, multiplied. Bricolage Grotesque for display and text; Barlow Condensed caps only for box labels and form labels. Current page marked with a yellow arrow, never a hue swap. Long reading sits on white with colour only at the edges.

STORY: the visitor sees a real person and a real place, understands the four stamped steps and the prices, and acts: fills the credential (service, modality) and the yellow arrow opens WhatsApp, or writes through the form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: on a Camino-blue field, the credential opened flat in three folded panels with visible fold shading: wide panel with "INALA Centro de Psicoterapia" at display scale and "Te acompaño en tu proceso terapéutico."; narrow panel with María's real photo and the aqua stamp overlapping its corner; booking panel with Servicio select, Modalidad radios and the yellow arrow "Reserva tu cita" (primary action, above the fold at 1280×720 and on a 390px phone after the headline). On phones the panels unfold vertically.

FORM: Credencial del Camino, position 1 on the ordered list (chosen as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over assigned 4). Seed key f713bc1e. Signature interaction: the four process steps are credential boxes that receive their stamp as they enter the viewport (CSS scroll-driven, visible by default, reduced-motion shows them stamped).
